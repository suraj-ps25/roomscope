# Benchmark report

Generated by `benchmark/report.py` from `benchmark/run_all.sh` outputs (commit `439719f`). Every number is
read from a metrics file; regenerate with `benchmark/run_all.sh`.

**What this is and isn't.** There are no captures from our own iPhone (none was available), so the
walk-in on real phone captures is still to be run (rehearsed by `scripts/walk_in.sh`), and the
head-to-head ran on a recording's raw cloud in a point-cloud tool (`benchmark/head_to_head/`, section
below). What is here: a synthetic flat with exact truth for
all three tiers (multi-room, stitching, openings, drift); real scanned rooms for the photo and video
tiers; the depth model's measured scale error on real iPad imagery; and real iPad Pro LiDAR recordings
of real rooms scored against laser scans, on dev and held-out visits. Gates are the brief's: openings ≤ 2 cm on
≥ 85% (misses and phantoms count), ceiling ≤ 1.5 cm, walls ±3% (video) / ±8% (photo), footprint ±8%.

**Stages and commits.** The real-capture stage (LiDAR tier on every recording, scored against laser truth) and the
assessors' sample captures were run at `2c9457e`. The synthetic, scanned-room, depth-scale and calibration stages
are from `ab4aa02`: the one pipeline change since, the sliver-wall absorption, leaves them unchanged (checked:
synthetic LiDAR, photo and video with true depth, and full-resolution damage give identical results), and the
real photo/video plans are rescored, not rerun (their layouts don't use the changed code).
