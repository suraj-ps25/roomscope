# Capture protocol (one page)

Pick **one** of A, B or C. **Before you start:** lights on, every interior door fully
open, nothing moved while you capture.

## A. LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or newer *Pro*, or iPad Pro): most accurate

1. Install **Stray Scanner** (free, App Store). In its settings set **15 fps**.
2. Stand in the first room, near the entrance. **Remember this spot.** Press record.
3. In **every** room:
   - In the **middle**, **turn slowly through one full circle** (~10 s). On the way
     round, tilt **up until you see the ceiling and down until you see the floor**, twice.
     In a small room, tilt well over.
   - Walk **slowly around the room ~1 m from the walls**, phone on the walls.
   - Go **through each doorway slowly** (2–3 s), phone pointing ahead.
4. Step into every space you can (hallway, cupboard, balcony). Where you can't step in
   (bath, shower), lean in and point the phone around.
5. **Finish at the spot from step 2**, facing the way you started. Hold still 3 s. Stop.
6. Hand-off: phone → Mac with a cable → Finder → the phone → *Files* → *Stray Scanner* →
   drag the newest folder to the Mac (or AirDrop it from the Files app).

About **1 minute per room**.

## B. Video (any iPhone 15 or newer)

1. Camera → **Video**, **1×**, phone **sideways (landscape)**.
2. **One clip** for the whole home. In **every** room: walk to the **middle**, **stop**,
   and **turn slowly through one full circle** (~10 s), tilting up to the ceiling and
   down to the floor twice, as in A step 3. Keep turning until you're past where you
   started. Then **walk normally** to the next room. **One turn per room.**
3. Hand-off: AirDrop the clip with **Options → All Photos Data** turned on.

About **20 seconds per room**.

## C. Photos (any iPhone 15 or newer)

1. Camera → **Photo**, **1×**, **landscape**. No Portrait mode, no zoom.
2. In **each room** take **5–8 photos**:
   - **4 corner photos:** from each corner, point at the **opposite corner**, at chest
     height, phone **nearly level**, with **the floor and the ceiling edge both in the
     picture**. If the ceiling edge is cut off, step further back into the corner.
   - **At every doorway:** stand **on the threshold** and take **one photo into each room,
     back to back**, each aimed across that room and **tilted up a little**. Each photo
     goes into its own room's folder. These join the rooms together, so never skip them.
3. Hand-off: on the Mac make **one folder per room** (`kitchen`, `bedroom`, `hallway`…)
   with that room's photos. AirDrop with **Options → All Photos Data** on.

About **1 minute per room**.

## Avoid

- Running, or fast turns: blur ruins video and photos.
- Covering the lens, or zooming.
- Closed doors: they look like wall.
- Skipping the ceiling: tilt up once per room.

Mirrors, glass, shiny floors and dim rooms need nothing special.

Then, on the Mac: double-click **RoomScope.command**, drop the folder (or video) on the page that
opens, press **Make the floor plan**. (Or: `roomscope run <capture> --out runs/<name>`.)
What happens in difficult cases: [`capture_notes.md`](capture_notes.md).
